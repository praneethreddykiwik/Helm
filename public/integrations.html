Email invites

Helm e-mails teammate invites (with a Join link) and, when you switch them on, client payment reminders and follow-ups. Set the name clients see and where their replies go.

Checking…

Shown as the "From" name. Empty = your studio name.
When a client presses Reply, it goes here. Empty = replies go to your studio's business e-mail (Settings), else to the studio owner's e-mail.

Changing the reply-to address needs two-step sign-in on your account. The sender name saves without it.

Only people who can edit Settings can change these settings.

How email works and how to set it up

For your studio (nothing to set up)

  1. Open Settings → User control (or the Staff page) and invite someone by e-mail.
  2. Helm e-mails the invite within about 2 minutes. If it does not arrive, ask them to check spam, then press Resend email (once every 10 minutes).
  3. The e-mail says which studio and role it is for. Client e-mails show your Sender name; replies go to your Reply-to e-mail (set both above, then press Send test e-mail to me).

WhatsApp

Proposals, approval codes, payment and event reminders and crew assignments - sent from your studio's own WhatsApp Business number.

Checking…

The number your clients know your studio by (international format, e.g. +91 98765 43210). Test messages go to this number from your connected WhatsApp number. Only a studio admin can send one, and the number must be the mobile saved in your (or a teammate's) Helm profile.
Choose which messages go out

Only people who can edit Settings can change the number. Settings → Notifications

Step-by-step: connect WhatsApp

A. Meta (about 1–3 days, mostly waiting for verification)

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and create (or open) your studio's Meta Business account. Business Settings → Business info → start Business verification (GST certificate or incorporation document).
  2. Go to developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create app → type Business → link it to that business. Inside the app choose Add product → WhatsApp.
  3. WhatsApp → API Setup → Add phone number. Use a number that is NOT on the normal WhatsApp phone app. Verify it by SMS or call and set the display name (your studio's name – Meta reviews it).
  4. On the same page copy the Phone number ID (a long number under the phone number – not the phone number itself) and the WhatsApp Business Account ID.
  5. Make a permanent token: Business Settings → Users → System users → Add (Admin) → Assign assets (the app and the WhatsApp account, full control) → Generate new token → expiry Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management. The 24-hour token on the API Setup page will NOT work.
  6. WhatsApp Manager → Payment settings: add a card. Meta charges per message (roughly ₹0.12 for a reminder or code in India).

B. In Helm (this card)

  1. Under Your WhatsApp number, paste the two IDs and the token and press Connect WhatsApp. No token yet? Press Save details – the two IDs stay saved and are filled in next time. Helm stores the token in its secure key store and never shows it again (Replace token puts in a new one) – never paste it anywhere else (not in chat or e-mail).
  2. Helm checks the number with Meta straight away. Connected shows your name and number; otherwise the line says what to fix.
  3. Press Set up message templates. Setup runs in the background – you can leave the page. Helm creates its 21 message templates in your WhatsApp account (using Meta's ready-made library wording where it fits), Meta reviews each one (minutes to a few hours) and you get one bell notification when they are all approved, or when one is rejected – with Meta's reason and a Fix and resubmit button. The approval-code template needs a verified business: verify it in Meta Business Manager; approval codes go by e-mail meanwhile.
  4. Save your studio WhatsApp number below and press Send test WhatsApp – it arrives from your connected number.
  5. In Settings → Notifications → Automatic client messages & WhatsApp, switch on the messages you want (payment reminders, follow-ups, WhatsApp copies of notifications). Each crew member can tick Also send my Helm notifications to my WhatsApp in their profile.

Good to know

  1. Until a number is connected, your studio sends no WhatsApp messages. E-mail, Copy link and the manual WhatsApp buttons keep working, and clients get their approval code by e-mail.
  2. Anyone who replies STOP is never messaged again until they reply START.
  3. Disconnect removes the token from Helm and frees the number – you can connect it again later.

Online payments (Razorpay)

Clients pay advances and balances by a payment link. The money goes straight into your own Razorpay account.

Checking…

Step-by-step: connect Razorpay
  1. Sign up at dashboard.razorpay.com and finish KYC (PAN, bank account, business documents).
  2. Start in Test mode: Account & Settings → API keys → Generate test key. Keep the Key ID (rzp_test_…) and the Key Secret.
  3. Send the keys to Helm support through a password-manager share. Helm stores them as secrets, deploys the payment functions and gives you a webhook address.
  4. In Razorpay → Webhooks → Add: paste that address, make up a long secret (give the same secret to Helm), tick payment_link.paid.
  5. Helm sends a ₹1 test link. When it shows as paid in Helm, repeat with Live keys.

Until this is on, you can still record payments by hand on each event (cash, UPI, bank transfer).
